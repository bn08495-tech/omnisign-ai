ENGINEERING & CAPABILITIES

How OmniSign AI Operates

Our custom logic parses natural speech and physical gestures to achieve low-latency bidirectional translation.

VOICE TO SIGN

Speech Tokenizer & Video Asset Synthesizer

Captures spoken voice in real time and decomposes speech into phonetic tokens. Matches keywords against our curated sign video database, automatically falling back to precise letter-by-letter fingerspelling sequences (e.g. "HI" → "H" + "I").

  • Real-time continuous speech transcription
  • Word-level greedy asset search with letter fallback
  • Frame-accurate playback buffer & timeline
SIGN TO VOICE

Fine-Tuned CV & Hand Position Classifier

Leverages MediaPipe, OpenCV, and TensorFlow fine-tuned on custom and open-source sign language datasets. Detects 21 hand landmarks and postures to recognize gestures, frame full sentences, and speak them aloud via natural TTS.

  • 21 3D hand keypoints with spatial rotation analysis
  • Character & word accumulation buffer
  • Instant neural text-to-speech audio playback
BIDIRECTIONAL BRIDGE

Two-Way Synchronous Telepresence Room

The live meeting engine that harmonizes both pipelines into one screen. Vocal speakers talk naturally and see translated sign visual streams; deaf or mute participants sign to their camera and hear their words articulated instantly.

  • Unified live video & sign projection stream
  • Real-time synchronized chat log & transcripts
  • Session analytics & latency telemetry
